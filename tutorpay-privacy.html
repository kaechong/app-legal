TutorPay Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

1. Data We Collect

TutorPay does not operate any servers and does not collect your data. All information you enter into the app — including student names, lesson schedules, attendance and payment records — is stored only on your device and, if you enable iCloud, in your personal iCloud account (using Apple's CloudKit). We, the developer, cannot access this data.

2. How Data Is Used

Your data is used solely within the app to provide its features: lesson management, fee tracking, automatic payment reminders, e-receipts and lesson rescheduling. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with any third party. If you choose to send a payment reminder, the app opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message; sending it is entirely your action and is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

4. Subscriptions

Paid subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You may delete any record within the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its local data; data stored in your iCloud can be managed or deleted through your Apple ID settings.

6. Children

TutorPay is a productivity tool for tutors and teachers. It is not directed at children.

7. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version will always be posted on this page. Questions: appchallengek@gmail.com

TutorPay 私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日

1. 我們收集的資料

TutorPay 沒有營運任何伺服器，亦不會收集你的資料。你在 App 內輸入的一切資料——包括學生姓名、課堂時間、出席及收費記錄——只會儲存在你的裝置，以及（如你啟用 iCloud）你的個人 iCloud 帳戶（經 Apple CloudKit）。我們作為開發者無法存取這些資料。

2. 資料用途

你的資料只會在 App 內用於其功能：課堂管理、收費追蹤、自動追數提醒、電子收據及請假順延。App 內沒有廣告、沒有分析工具、沒有第三方追蹤。

3. 資料分享

我們不會出售、出租或與任何第三方分享你的資料。如你選擇發送追數提醒，App 會開啟 WhatsApp 並預填訊息；是否發送完全由你決定，並受 WhatsApp 私隱政策約束。

4. 訂閱付款

付費訂閱由 Apple 經 App Store 處理。我們不會接收或儲存你的信用卡資料。

5. 資料保留與刪除

你可隨時在 App 內刪除任何記錄。刪除 App 會移除其本地資料；存放在 iCloud 的資料可經你的 Apple ID 設定管理或刪除。

6. 兒童

TutorPay 是供補習老師使用的工具，並非以兒童為對象。

7. 修訂與聯絡

我們或會不時更新本政策，最新版本會一直張貼於本頁。查詢：appchallengek@gmail.com
