RetireTrack Support

RetireTrack tracks how you are doing against your retirement plan: a 30-second monthly close compares your actual net worth with the plan's path and shows how far ahead or behind you are.

The app is currently available in Traditional Chinese only.

FAQ

How do I do a monthly close?

On the Overview tab, tap "Start month close". Pro: confirm automatic entries → add other changes → update holding prices → see the summary. Free: view the month's summary and tap "Finish" (update prices yourself on the Holdings tab).

Which month does a close cover?

Pick a reminder day in Settings › Monthly close reminder (default: the 25th at 8 pm). A day from the 1st to the 14th reviews the previous month; the 15th or later (for example payday) closes the current month.

How are "ahead" and "behind" calculated?

The app works out, month by month, how much your plan (including life events) says you should have today and compares it with your actual net worth. Every formula is explained under Plan › Assumptions.

Do prices and exchange rates update automatically?

No. RetireTrack does not fetch quotes from the internet; you type in prices and exchange rates (close step 3, the Holdings tab, or Settings › Exchange rates).

How do I keep my data on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID with iCloud on and your data syncs automatically. You can also export CSV files in Settings › Data.

How do I restore or cancel a subscription?

Restore: Settings › Subscription › Restore Purchases, or at the bottom of the paywall. Cancel: Settings › Subscription › Manage Subscription, or iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the free trial ends, the plan you chose is charged.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free: one plan, plan tracking, quick entry, manual monthly close, basic charts, one life event and ledger CSV export. Pro (HK$18 a month or HK$128 a year, first 7 days free): automatic recurring entries, the monthly close wizard, unlimited life events and full CSV import and export.

How do I delete all my data?

Settings › Delete all data and start over. With iCloud sync on, the copy in your iCloud is deleted too.
