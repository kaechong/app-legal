RetireTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

In short: we do not collect your data

RetireTrack has no server, no account, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone and, if you are signed in to iCloud, is synced to your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). We, the developer, cannot see or access this data, and we never sell, analyse or share it with anyone.

What the app stores

DataExamplesWhere

RetireTrack does not read your contacts, photos, location, health data or clipboard, and never connects to your bank or brokerage. Prices and exchange rates are typed in by you; the app does not fetch quotes from the internet.

Network use

The app only talks to Apple services: the App Store (subscriptions and restore) and iCloud / CloudKit (syncing your own data). It sends nothing to any other server. Tapping Privacy Policy, Support or Terms opens that web page in your browser without attaching any data.

Subscriptions

RetireTrack Pro monthly and annual subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app only receives Apple's transaction record to unlock features on your device; we never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

Monthly close reminder

If you allow notifications, your iPhone shows a local reminder at 8 pm on the day you choose. It contains no amounts or personal data, and we never push notifications from a server. Turn it off in the app (Settings › Monthly close reminder) or in iPhone Settings › Notifications.

CSV export and import

Export creates CSV files in the app's own temporary folder and hands them to the place or app you pick in the share sheet. Import reads only the file you pick in the file browser. We never receive these files.

Deleting your data

  • Settings › Delete all data and start over removes every plan, entry, holding, rule and monthly close on the device; with iCloud sync on, the deletion syncs to your iCloud.
  • Deleting the app alone does not remove its iCloud data; remove it in iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.
  • To stop syncing, turn off RetireTrack in iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud.

Children

RetireTrack is for adults planning their retirement. It is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Important

Projections and simulations in this app are for reference only and do not constitute investment or financial advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and mention it in the app's release notes.
