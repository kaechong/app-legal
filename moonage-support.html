Common questions

Restore a purchase: Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchase". Your full-version unlock is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

iCloud sync: Sync and family sharing need you to be signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled for MoonAge. After signing in, open the app and give it a moment to download your records.

Family sharing: The baby's owner invites family from the sharing screen. Invited members use their own Apple IDs and follow the baby owner's plan: if the owner has the full version, members enjoy full-version limits too.

Monthly free cards: The free version includes 5 cards per month, resetting on the 1st. Watching a rewarded ad earns 1 extra card each time.

常見問題

恢復購買：在 App 內開啟「設定」並點按「恢復購買」。完整版解鎖跟你的 Apple ID，在任何以同一 Apple ID 登入的裝置都可恢復。

iCloud 同步：同步及家庭共享需要你已登入 iCloud，並為 MoonAge 開啟 iCloud Drive。登入後開啟 App，稍等片刻即可下載你的記錄。

家庭共享：由寶寶記錄的擁有人在共享畫面邀請家人。被邀請的成員使用自己的 Apple ID，額度跟隨擁有人：擁有人持有完整版時，成員同樣享有完整版上限。

每月免費卡片：免費版每月有 5 張卡，每月 1 號重置。觀看一次獎勵廣告可多得 1 張卡。
