MoonAge Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

1. Data We Collect

MoonAge does not operate any servers and does not collect your data. Everything you enter in the app — your baby's name, birth date, photos and the milestone cards you create — is stored only on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your personal iCloud account (via Apple CloudKit). We, as the developer, cannot access this data.

2. Photos

Photos you pick are composed into cards on your device. They are never uploaded to us. When you use iCloud sync or family sharing, photo data travels only between your devices and the people you invite, through Apple's CloudKit, under your own Apple ID.

3. Family Sharing

If you invite family members to a baby's record, those people can view and edit that record through Apple's CloudKit sharing, using their own Apple IDs. You can stop sharing at any time in the app. This is a feature of your iCloud account; we never see the shared content.

4. Advertising (Free Version)

The free version shows optional rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob — you choose whether to watch an ad to earn an extra card. To serve ads, Google may collect device information, an advertising identifier (only with your consent under Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), IP address and ad interaction data, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. The full (paid) version contains no ads.

5. Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment details.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete any record in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its local data; data stored in iCloud can be managed or deleted through your Apple ID settings.

7. Children

MoonAge is made for parents and caregivers to record their babies' growth. It is intended for use by adults and is not directed at children.

8. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version will always be posted on this page. Questions: appchallengek@gmail.com

MoonAge 私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日

1. 我們收集的資料

MoonAge 沒有營運任何伺服器，亦不會收集你的資料。你在 App 內輸入的一切資料——包括寶寶姓名、出生日期、相片及你製作的成長卡片——只會儲存在你的裝置，以及（如你使用 iCloud）你的個人 iCloud 帳戶（經 Apple CloudKit）。我們作為開發者無法存取這些資料。

2. 相片

你選取的相片只會在你的裝置上合成為卡片，絕不會上傳給我們。使用 iCloud 同步或家庭共享時，相片資料只會經 Apple CloudKit、以你自己的 Apple ID，在你的裝置與你邀請的人之間傳送。

3. 家庭共享

如你邀請家人加入寶寶的記錄，對方可用自己的 Apple ID 經 Apple CloudKit 共享查看及編輯該記錄。你可隨時在 App 內停止共享。這是你 iCloud 帳戶的功能；我們不會看到共享的內容。

4. 廣告（免費版）

免費版會提供由 Google AdMob 提供的自選獎勵廣告——是否觀看廣告以換取額外卡片，完全由你決定。為提供廣告，Google 可能會收集裝置資料、廣告識別碼（只在你於 Apple「App 追蹤透明度」提示中同意後）、IP 位址及廣告互動資料，並受 Google 私隱政策約束。完整版（付費）不含任何廣告。

5. 購買

完整版為一次性購買，由 Apple 經 App Store 處理。我們不會接收或儲存你的付款資料。

6. 資料保留與刪除

你可隨時在 App 內刪除任何記錄。刪除 App 會移除其本地資料；存放在 iCloud 的資料可經你的 Apple ID 設定管理或刪除。

7. 兒童

MoonAge 是供家長及照顧者記錄寶寶成長的工具，對象為成人，並非以兒童為對象。

8. 修訂與聯絡

我們或會不時更新本政策，最新版本會一直張貼於本頁。查詢：appchallengek@gmail.com
